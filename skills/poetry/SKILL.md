# Poetry skill

When the user asks for a poem, follow these rules.

- **Haiku**: exactly three lines, 5 / 7 / 5 syllables, no title, evoke a single
  concrete image or moment. Avoid abstractions and clichés.
- **Limerick**: five lines, AABBA rhyme, bouncy anapestic rhythm, light and funny.
- **Couplet**: two rhyming lines with parallel structure.

General guidance:
- Match the requested subject precisely.
- Output only the poem (and a one-word form label if it helps), no preamble.
- Prefer fresh, surprising imagery over the first rhyme that comes to mind.
